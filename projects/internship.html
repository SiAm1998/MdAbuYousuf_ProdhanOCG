About the Internship

At the entrance of Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM), Penang during the research internship.

I completed a two-week research internship at the Centre for Policy Research (CPR), Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM) in Penang from 1–14 February 2026, through an academic collaboration between USM and the Department of Oceanography, Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST). The internship provided hands-on exposure to marine governance, blue carbon, coastal ecosystems, and policy-oriented environmental research.

The internship was particularly valuable in connecting my background in oceanography and ecosystem modelling with the broader challenge of translating scientific evidence into practical policy recommendations. It complemented my research interests in coastal ecosystems and marine governance while giving me experience working in an international, multidisciplinary research environment.

The Project

What We Worked On

The internship was centred on the collaborative project “Designing a Marine Governance Strategy Grounded in Seagrass and Blue Carbon Evidence.” The broader objective was to develop science-informed recommendations for sustainable coastal management, with a particular focus on Pulau Gazumbo and surrounding coastal systems in Penang.

As part of the team, I contributed to field-based ecological assessments of seagrass beds, mangrove ecosystems, and intertidal environments at Middle Bank and Pulau Gazumbo. Additionally, on short notice, I delivered a key academic presentation titled "Application of an Ecosystem-Based ATN Model for MPA Assessment in Penang, Malaysia," sharing modeling frameworks relevant to local marine protected area planning.

Tools / Methods used: Field ecological assessment | Intertidal observation | Google Earth Engine (GEE) | NDRE remote sensing | GIS/spatial assessment | Ecosystem modeling (ATN) | Policy-brief development

Remote Sensing

Seagrass Assessment via NDRE

Normalized Difference Red Edge (NDRE) analysis executed on Google Earth Engine to detect annual canopy and density changes in coastal seagrass beds.

To evaluate the current ecological state of local seagrass habitats, I performed spatial time-series analysis using Google Earth Engine (GEE). By applying the Normalized Difference Red Edge (NDRE) index, we mapped the annual trajectory and vegetation stress levels to monitor seagrass decline across two key ecological sites: Pulau Gazumbo and Middle Bank.

Workflow

Field Reporting & Communication

Our routine balanced intensive field data collection with systematic analytical logging. To maintain rigorous data integration between field observations and policy synthesis, we compiled and submitted daily field reports tracking sampling progress, environmental conditions, and spatial findings.
